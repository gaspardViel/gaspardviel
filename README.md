### About Me

I'm a social worker and sector manager at **LogiVitae**, a home care service in Paris, where I coordinate care workers and support vulnerable clients. Alongside that, I build the apps we use.

Social work in France is far from the cutting edge of technology, even though the people we serve need all the help they can get. We lose hours to badly organised information systems, partial views of our own data, and tools nobody can bend to the actual job.

AI gives practitioners access to programming. Learning to use it well can do a lot for organisations where every euro counts, because every euro saved goes back into supporting people.

I've been doing prompt engineering since the early days of ChatGPT, and I now work mostly with agents. Give an agent a good working environment and a clear grasp of what LLMs can and can't do, and it can go a very long way in the world of text.
